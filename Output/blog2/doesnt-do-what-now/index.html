Doesn't Do What Now?

November 19, 2026

Before we started watching some terrible football Saturday night, there was a basketball game during the day. I thought it was a little bit funny that LSU was paid to go up to a small school's home site to presumably lose. LSU as someone else's rent-a-win.

But remember when the Coach O tigers lost to Troy? Or way back when I was in school, Colorado State came to Baton Rouge and took home a football win along with their big game check? Well, in basketball, the Tigers were the ones who collected both a check and a win.

I went to the eye doctor yesterday, and Doc was pretty fired up about that game. We touched on the awful play of the football tigers and Saints this weekend, and then he broke down what a fun team we were going to have this year. And then he went into the recruiting class, which has never ever been ranked #1.

I remember that in his first stint, Will Wade's teams always played hard. But I guess when the checkbook doesn't have to be hidden, he can recruit a little also.

Dr. A and I went through all of this, and he was pretty much wrapping up when I thought to ask, "and how are my eyes?"

"Exactly the same. Here are your prescriptions." And that was that.

At work, I have an AI agent on my desktop, and I feed it tasks that would either require me to google syntax or dig through log files or other tedious things and spit out answers. This week, I had a process that was stuck on a particular step, and when I looked at that code, I couldn't see that it really even did anything. I asked the AI agent, and it's picking up my way of speaking to it.


The blurred bit is just a long file path. I don't think showing it would give away any big secrets, but better to be safe.
