Going through old posts, I'm surprised about how much I used to post about work.  I didn't realize back in the 00's that I worked in a uniquely silly place with people good at their jobs who shared my humor.  Here's a post where we return to workplace stories.  I don't think it will be a habit.

This morning, I went to work without my phone.  It was on purpose, but I didn't think about the fact that a good bit of what I needed to do was behind a 2-factor authentication that was only tied to an alert on my phone.

I contacted big corporate support early, hoping they have a way to set me up another authentication method.    I got back an automated email with a link to a Sharepoint page that answers most of the questions.

I followed that link, and got the message that I don't have permission on that page.  But it did have a box where I could explain my interest and request access.  I filled that out like the good little corporate stooge.

An hour later, I got another automated email:
