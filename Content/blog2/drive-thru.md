Friday morning, Ko and I were waiting in the drive-thru lane at Starbucks.  The car in front of us was really getting on his nerves, waving around their phone at the driver.  I guess they were trying to pay or get reward points or some such thing.

"You dare me to pull out my phone and just start showing her pictures when we get up there?"

He's not amused by such shenanigans.

I pulled out my phone and took a look at the last picture I saved:

Then the thought of showing this to the barista for no appearent reason really got me tickled.  

When Ko threw his sandwich wrapper on the floor, I told him that I was going to pull up at school right in front of the high school principal, and just toss the balled up bag out on the school lawn.  Then I would just look right into his eyes like a crazy man.

This got me laughing all over again.
