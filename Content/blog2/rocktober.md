Back at the beginning of October, I decided that I would try to listen to a new album every day.  It could be something where I know the radio hits, but not something where I knew it as a complete work.  I didn't even nearly cover one a day, although if you count the ones I put on and then fell asleep to, the list is considerably longer.  At any rate, here are my thoughts on 9 albums that I hadn't heard at this time last month.
